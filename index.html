Common questions

How much does it cost?

Karot is free to download. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. After that, Carrot is £1.49 a month or £9.99 a year, and Carrot + Rewire is £2.99 a month or £19.99 a year (UK prices; other countries pay the local equivalent). Plans renew automatically until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Karot. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a plan.

What happens when my plan ends?

The shield switches off. Nothing stays locked. Your lists and history stay on your phone, ready if you choose a plan again.

I bought a plan but the app asks again

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought the plan with.

Does Karot see which apps I block?

No. Apple's Screen Time picker gives the app opaque tokens, not app names. Karot never sends Screen Time data off your iPhone. When you set your baseline, it shows your average time in your chosen apps; iOS works that out in a sealed extension that cannot connect to the internet, and Karot does not store it.

What does Rewire send, and to whom?

Only if you use Rewire and agree in the app: your sheet photo, your goal, your first name if you typed one, your affirmation and your recent tied tasks, with whether you ticked them, go through Karot's own server to OpenAI, which writes your options and checks the sheet. Karot's server does not keep or log what you send; it keeps only short-lived security and usage records, listed in the privacy policy. OpenAI may keep requests for up to 30 days to check for abuse and does not train on them. See the privacy policy.

How do I remove the shield completely?

Revoke Screen Time access for Carrot in Settings, Screen Time, or delete the app.
